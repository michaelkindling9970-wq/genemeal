  1. Open the 23andMe download page

    23andMe does not offer an API for other apps. You request the file in your own account. This button opens their raw-data tool in a new tab.

    Open 23andMe raw data

    https://you.23andme.com/tools/data/

  2. Request the file, then wait for their email

    On that page, confirm your date of birth, tick the checkbox, and submit. 23andMe emails you when the raw data download is ready. People often see that note in about a week. It can take up to 30 days.

    Two times are stored: about 7 days from now and about 30 days from now. Mail is not sent unless REMINDER_EMAIL_ENABLED=1, and even then this build only logs a stub. It does not deliver email.
