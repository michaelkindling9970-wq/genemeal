What to write to us about

  • Questions or feedback about GeneMeal, your file, or your menu.
  • Privacy and data requests, including asking what we hold about you or asking us to delete it.
  • Billing questions. Please don't send card numbers or other payment details by email.

Please don't email your DNA file or genetic results. We don't need them to help you.

Do it yourself, right now

  • Delete your data: use Delete my data on the guide page. It removes your encrypted upload, marker notes, menu and saved progress from our server straight away.
  • Cancel your subscription: use the Cancel subscription page. Card subscriptions are managed in Stripe's customer portal, PayPal subscriptions under Automatic payments in your PayPal account.

If the self-serve options don't work for you, for example because you cleared your cookies or you're in a state where GeneMeal isn't offered, email us and we'll help.
